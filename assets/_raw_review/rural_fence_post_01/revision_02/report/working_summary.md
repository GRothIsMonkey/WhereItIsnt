# Revision 02 — internal visual working summary, before modelling

1. Aim for grounded cinematic realism with a subtle stylized edge. Believable agricultural construction and tactile materials come before technical neatness.
2. Keep real human-scale proportions. Simplification may improve readability, but neither scan-like noise nor exaggerated low-poly forms belong here.
3. D1 begins as a familiar, earthy, exposed rural region. The Farm Compound includes fences, livestock areas, dirt paths and neglected working infrastructure; this piece must suggest a place that once functioned normally.
4. Keep ordinary objects ordinary. No supernatural marks, horror damage, expressive spikes or theatrical grime.
5. Give imperfections causes: sawing, drying, pressure at a joint, rain on end grain, and long-term edge wear. Do not perturb every vertex randomly.
6. Show a specific weathering sequence: serviceable old timber, a weakened housed joint, practical later steel reinforcement, then mild neglect. Retain warm underlying wood beneath gray sun bleaching.
7. Make silhouette and construction carry the design before textures: a softly uneven post crown, restrained bow/taper, a sagging rough-sawn rail, and readable shoulders at the joint.
8. Separate materials physically: diffuse dry fibre and broad wood roughness versus cooler galvanized steel, tighter highlights, rounded bends and modest zinc mottling.
9. Allocate detail by viewing distance. Use the expanded triangle allowance on profile, joint, top and meaningful checks; judge from 1, 5, 15 and 30 metres without claiming bolts remain legible at 30 m.
10. Coherence outranks an isolated attractive model. The asset must support WHERE IT ISN'T's authored rural world and must pass human visual review, not merely a budget/export check.
11. Establish reusable fence-family vocabulary: squared agricultural timber, housed rails, simple repair straps and common fasteners. Model only this one piece, with review-only linked repetitions.
12. Preserve portable PBR, two material families, metric placement, a base-centred origin and independent future collision boxes. Use approximately 64 px/m; improved close-range form must not be replaced by secretly increasing texture density.
